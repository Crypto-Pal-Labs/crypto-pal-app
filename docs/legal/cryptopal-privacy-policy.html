Crypto Pal — Privacy Policy

Crypto Pal is a non-custodial wallet. Your private keys are generated and stored locally on your device. We do not have access to your private keys, seed phrase, or funds.

We do not collect personal information by default. Optional diagnostics, if enabled, may include anonymous crash logs and basic analytics (app version, device model, screen views). You can opt out at any time.

Third‑party providers (e.g., Covalent, Infura/Alchemy, Transak, WalletConnect) operate under their privacy policies. When you use their features, you interact with them directly.
